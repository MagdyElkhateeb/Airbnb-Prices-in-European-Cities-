realSum : The total price of the Airbnb listing(الاعلان). (Numeric)
room_type: The type of room being offered (e.g. Entire home/apt, Private room, Shared room). (Categorical)
room_shared: Whether the room is shared or not. True if the room type is Shared only. (Boolean)
room_private: Whether the room is private or not. True if the room type is Private only.(Boolean)
person_capacity: The maximum number of people that can stay in the room. (Numeric)
host_is_superhost: Whether the host is a superhost or not.A Superhost is a host that has received a special rating from Airbnb based on the platform's criteria. (Boolean)
multi: Whether the listing is for multiple rooms or not. Does this listing apply to several identical rooms, or is there only one? (Boolean)
biz: Whether the listing is for business purposes or not. (Boolean)
cleanliness_rating: The cleanliness rating of the listing. (Numeric)
guest_satisfaction_overall: The overall guest satisfaction rating of the listing. (Numeric)
bedrooms: The number of bedrooms in the listing. (Numeric)
dist: The distance from the city centre. (Numeric)
metro_dist: The distance from the nearest metro station. (Numeric)
lng: The longitude of the listing. (Numeric)
lat: The latitude of the listing. (Numeric)
attr_index: The attraction index, which measures the availability and attractiveness of tourist attractions around the listing. Higher values generally indicate a greater presence of nearby tourist attractions. (Numeric)
attr_index_norm: The normalized attraction index, which represents the attraction index on a normalized scale to make values easier to compare. (Numeric)
rest_index: The restaurant index, which measures the availability and attractiveness of restaurants around the listing. Higher values generally indicate a greater presence of nearby restaurants. (Numeric)
rest_index_norm: The normalized restaurant index, which represents the restaurant index on a normalized scale to make values easier to compare. (Numeric)
